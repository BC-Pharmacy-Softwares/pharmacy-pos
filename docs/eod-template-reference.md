# End of Day Sign-Off — Regulatory Reference (BC / PODSA)

> Source of truth for the EOD checklist in `js/utils/checklists.js`.
> Provided by the pharmacy owner (Pill4Me). Mirrors the required PODSA sections.
> **Do not drop fields without confirming with the pharmacist** — these are compliance items.

## Header
- Pharmacist on Duty (RPh) — full name
- Pharmacy Assistant / Tech — full name
- Closing Time (auto = now, editable)

## SECURITY & PHYSICAL PREMISES — *PODSA s.26 — Required*
- Security camera system checked for proper operation — *s.26(1)(b)(ii); 30-day archive*
- Dispensary monitored alarm armed — *s.26(2)(a)(i)*
- Schedule I, II & controlled substances secured behind physical barriers — *s.26(2)(a)(ii)* **(RPh)**
- Schedule IA drugs confirmed in time-delay safe (min 5-min delay lock) — *s.26(1)(a)* **(RPh)**
- Cash counted, excess secured in safe, safe locked and confirmed
- All PHI secured — none left on counter, desk, or printer — *s.26(2)(a)(ii)*
- Dispensing counter cleared — no pending prescriptions left unattended
- Open sign confirmed OFF (smart plug auto-off verified or manually confirmed)

## COLD CHAIN TEMPERATURE LOG — *PODSA s.23.9 — Required Daily*
Must record at **opening AND closing**. Fridge **+2 to +8 °C**, Freezer **−25 to −10 °C**.
- Fridge — current temp (2–8 °C)
- Fridge — min today
- Fridge — max today
- Freezer — current temp (−25 to −10 °C)
- Temperature excursion today? (Yes/No)

## NARCOTICS, CONTROLLED & TARGETED SUBSTANCES — *PODSA s.23.8 — Required*
- Controlled/targeted/narcotic dispenses report printed from WinRx — RPh reviewed running balance, signed & filed in narcotic binder — *retain 3 yrs s.23.8(10)* **(RPh initials)**
- No unexplained discrepancies in narcotic running balance — *s.23.8(8)(c)*
- Narcotic storage area locked and secure
- Narcotic / controlled drug order received from wholesaler today? (Yes/No)

## WHOLESALER STOCK RECEIVING
- General wholesaler order received today? (Yes/No)

## DISPENSING & BLISTER PACKS
- Scheduled / auto-refill billing processed — no unprocessed billings remaining in WinRx queue
- Blister packs — accuracy checked, pharmacist final check completed and initialled **(RPh initials)**
- Uncollected prescriptions returned to will-call / filed appropriately
- Patient returns processed — non-reusable drugs set aside for destruction — *s.22*

## SYSTEMS & EQUIPMENT
- WinRx end-of-day process completed — daily reports generated and saved
- Fax / printer queue cleared — no prescription documents left in tray or on screen
- All workstation screens locked or logged out

Total Rx Dispensed · Closing Float ($) · Injections / Vaccines

Free text.
